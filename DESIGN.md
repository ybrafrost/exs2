## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the physical and aesthetic properties of precision-engineered architectural tempered glass: luminous clarity, structural rigor, optical refraction, and understated luxury. Designed for high-end residential homeowners, commercial developers, architects, and interior designers across Lima's coastal and urban environments, the visual atmosphere balances structural permanence with airy transparency.

The stylistic direction blends **Architectural Minimalism** with refined **Glassmorphism**:
- Generous structural whitespace echoing open-concept architectural floor plans.
- Translucent frosted glass planes (`backdrop-blur`) that convey light diffusion without sacrificing readability.
- Razor-sharp structural alignment paired with softened radii mimicking polished beveled glass edges.
- High-trust functional cues optimized for high-value architectural quotes and instant communication via regional channels (such as WhatsApp).

## Colors

The palette draws directly from architectural tempered glass panels set against structural steel and oceanic coastal light.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Brand (`#0B192C`):** Deep Architectural Navy. Represents heavy structural framework, hardware fittings, structural stability, and luxury authority. Used for prominent headers, brand anchors, primary action states, and high-contrast accents.
- **Secondary Accent (`#0284C7` / `#38BDF8` / `#BAE6FD`):** Ice Glass Blue gradient tier. Reflects optical transmission, treated UV coatings, and sky reflections. Used for interactive focal points, badges, subtle glow filters, and focus outlines.
- **Tertiary Conversion Accent (`#601869`):** Deep Magenta/Violet Accent. Used for targeted focal points, specialized badges, and dynamic highlights within the system.
- **Surfaces & Grounds:**
  - Base Light Canvas: Pristine White (`#FFFFFF`) with secondary atmospheric planes in Cool Soft Gray (`#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`).
  - Dark Mode & Architectural Showcases: Deep Navy (`#0A1128` / `#0F172A`).
  - Glass Layering: Translucent frosted white `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)` on light layers and `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.65)` on dark tiers.
- **Borders & Seams:** Cool Slate Frost (`#E2E8F0`) on solid surfaces, transitioning to translucent frosted seams (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35)` or `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`) on glass panels.
- **Text & Contrast:** Deep Graphite Slate (`#0F172A`) for effortless legibility, supported by Slate Muted (`#64748B`) for technical specifications and metadata.

## Typography

Typographic hierarchy relies exclusively on **Manrope** for its precision geometry, architectural balance, open apertures, and modern aesthetic. 

- **Display & Headlines:** Tightly tracked headings (-0.03em to -0.01em) provide muscular structural presence that mimics carved stone and metallic framework.
- **Body:** Renders with comfortable line spacing (1.6x) in neutral slate tones to ensure high readability of technical glass thicknesses (8mm, 10mm, 12mm), acoustic ratings, and architectural specifications.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Uppercase label roles (`label-caps`) employ deliberate positive letter spacing (+0.08em) for categorical tags, architectural project labels, and product status badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a flexible 12-column architectural grid system designed to simulate spatial modularity, structural rhythm, and rhythmic paneling.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with 24px gutters, max-width bounded container at 1320px, and outer margins of 40px (`2.5rem`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 32px outer margins.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4-column layout with 16px gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) outer margins.

### Vertical Cadence & Spatial Rhythm
Component layouts follow a disciplined 8px base rhythm. Content modules breathe with ample negative space (`space-xl` and section paddings up to `5rem`), establishing a clean, premium, and clutter-free gallery aesthetic suited for architectural photography.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects harsh, opaque drop shadows in favor of ambient light transmission, translucent layering, and subtle optical dispersion mimicking real glass.

### Surface Tiers & Frosted Glass Treatment
1. **Base Surface:** Flat, neutral white (`#FFFFFF`) or ultra-light tint (`#F8FAFC`).
2. **Frosted Translucent Tier (`Glass Card`):** 
   - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)` on light; `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.65)` on dark.
   - Backdrop Filter: `blur(16px) saturate(180%)`.
   - Structural Border: 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)` top/left edge light reflex, transitioning to `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.6)` on bottom/right.
3. **Ambient Elevation Shadows:**
   - **Level 1 (Hover states, subtle cards):** `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.05)`.
   - **Level 2 (Dropdowns, floating navigational bars):** `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.08), 0 2px 6px 0 rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.02)`.
   - **Level 3 (Modals, quotation configurator panels):** `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.14), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.1)`.
4. **Active Edge Reflex:** Selected or prioritized cards incorporate an inner stroke or subtle top highlight mimicking a polished glass chamfer (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`).

## Shapes

The geometric vocabulary balances architectural precision with tactile comfort. A base scale of `roundedness: 2` delivers smooth, refined contours:

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Form fields, interactive chips, badges, and tertiary buttons.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-lg` - `1rem` / 16px):** Floating toolbars, product detail panels, dropdown menus, and standard cards.
- **Extra Large Radius (`rounded-xl` - `1.5rem` / 24px):** Primary architectural project cards, hero showcase banners, and quotation modals. Emulates the curved bevel of rounded-corner tempered architectural glass balustrades.
- **Pill Contours:** Reserved exclusively for conversational micro-actions (floating WhatsApp consultation triggers and category pill-filters).

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Architectural Button:** Solid Deep Navy (`#0B192C`) with white text, crisp 8px radius, height of 48px, bold tracking. Hover produces a subtle upward elevation shift and a refined glow: `box-shadow: 0 8px 20px -4px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.25)`.
- **Secondary Glass Button:** Translucent ice finish (`rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.08)`), border 1px solid `rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.3)`, text in Sky/Navy (`#0284C7`). Hover intensifies background tint to `rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.15)`.
- **Conversion CTA / Accent Button:** Vibrant Magenta/Violet Accent (`#601869`), white typography, high contrast, accompanied by a dedicated messaging icon or highlight state.

### Cards & Project Showcases
- **Architectural Showcase Card:** Features `rounded-xl` (24px) corners, frosted translucent caption overlays (`backdrop-blur: 12px`), 1px frosted boundary, and smooth zoom on contained architectural imagery (`transform: scale(1.03)` with a 500ms easing curve).
- **Specification Matrix Card:** Crisp white surface with light gray baseline (`#F1F5F9`), highlighting technical specs such as glass thickness, acoustic reduction (dB), and hardware system compatibility.

### Form Inputs & Quotation Calculators
- **Inputs:** 48px height, 8px radius, border in `#E2E8F0`, surface in `#FFFFFF`. 
- **Focus State:** 1px stroke shift to Ice Blue (`#0284C7`) with an outer frosted halo ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.25)`.
- **Labels:** Crisp, uppercase label format (`label-caps`) set in `#64748B` anchored 6px above input fields.

### Chips & Filter Tabs
- **Glass Filter Chips:** Segmented pill and 8px rounded options with subtle backdrop blur. Unselected states blend into the canvas with soft `#64748B` text. Selected states flip to `#0B192C` background with crisp white typography.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Geometric square (checkbox) and circular (radio) controls with an inset border of 1.5px `#CBD5E1`. Active state fills with `#0284C7` bearing an optical crisp white SVG icon, reinforced by a glowing ring highlight on keyboard navigation.

### Specialized Architectural Components
- **Glass Thickness Selector:** Interactive segmented cards highlighting cross-section diagrams with tactile dimension indicators (8mm, 10mm, 12mm, laminated).
- **Before / After Architectural Slider:** Frosted glass sliding divider line with ice-blue drag pill allowing side-by-side comparison between open-frame and installed tempered partitions.
